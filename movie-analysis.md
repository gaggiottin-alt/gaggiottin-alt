# Movie Performance Analysis

**Tools:** Python, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, scatter plots, bar charts, boxplots.

An undergraduate exploratory analysis of more than 85,000 movie records examining budget, revenue, genres, runtimes and audience engagement across approximately 25 years. The goal was to visualize correlations and patterns, not to make causal claims about movie success.
