# Customer Shopping Behavior and Segmentation

**Tools:** R, RStudio, tidyverse, ggplot2, caret, factoextra, K-Means, hierarchical clustering, DBSCAN.

This analysis uses 3,900 records and 16 customer attributes, including age, spending, ratings, and previous purchases. It performs exploratory analysis, normalizes features, and explores clustering strategies. An exploratory four-cluster K-Means solution had mean purchase amounts of $81.80, $60.40, $58.80 and $37.80.

**Interpretation note:** The silhouette diagnostic shown in the analysis suggests that other choices of K should also be investigated; this four-group solution is illustrative, not a demonstrated global optimum.
